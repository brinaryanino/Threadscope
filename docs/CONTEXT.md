# ThreadScope

ThreadScope is the domain of collecting, tracking, and interpreting the owner's Threads account performance to identify content patterns associated with account growth.

## Language

**Owner**:
The single person who operates ThreadScope and connects their own Threads account during the personal-use release.
_Avoid_: Customer, beta user, tenant

**Personal-use release**:
The initial product stage in which ThreadScope serves one Owner and is not distributed as a private beta or public SaaS.
_Avoid_: Private beta, public launch

**Micro-SaaS**:
A narrowly scoped web product centered on Threads growth intelligence, designed so it may later become a small commercial SaaS after personal validation.
_Avoid_: Enterprise platform, agency suite

**Threads Account**:
The Owner's account on Meta Threads connected to ThreadScope through the official Threads API.
_Avoid_: Social account, profile

**Account Growth**:
The measured change in supported account-level metrics over a defined period, without claiming that any individual post directly caused the change.
_Avoid_: Follower attribution

**Potential Growth Driver**:
A post or content pattern associated with a subsequent growth signal, presented with evidence and limitations rather than as causal attribution.
_Avoid_: Growth cause, guaranteed driver

**Snapshot**:
A time-stamped record of supported account or post metrics used to build history from the point tracking begins.
_Avoid_: Live metric, historical import

**Calculated Metric**:
An authoritative statistic derived deterministically from stored data using a documented, tested formula.
_Avoid_: AI metric

**Generated Insight**:
A human-readable interpretation or recommendation grounded in Calculated Metrics and linked evidence.
_Avoid_: Calculated metric, fact
